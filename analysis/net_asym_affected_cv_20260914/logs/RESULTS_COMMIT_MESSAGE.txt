analysis: affected-county CV round write-up, README and checksums

Pre-registered decisions (Delta = MSE_NET - MSE_ASYM; component sign-flip p < 0.05 with a
consistent sign):

- All 26 events are undecided, both on affected windows (-3.89e-04, p = 0.11) and on all
  windows (-2.77e-04, p = 0.21).
- Convective and flood events support ASYM on all windows (+7.09e-05, p = 0.024) but are
  undecided on affected windows.
- Wind and tropical events lean NET (affected -1.60e-03, p = 0.063, five of seven events)
  and ASYM is worse than persistence in the three largest of them.
- Winter is undecided, with six of seven events favouring ASYM.
- Unaffected counties support ASYM because both models predict spurious outages there and
  ASYM's are smaller; both are far worse than persistence.

RMSE agrees in direction everywhere. All 50 final checkpoints replay bit-exactly.
